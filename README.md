# Firehawk Aerospace

Firehawk Aerospace, Inc. is a Dallas, Texas defense-technology manufacturer founded in 2019 by Ronald Jones, Will Edwards and Steve Edwards that replaces conventional rocket-propulsion production with additive manufacturing. Its proprietary 3D-printed solid rocket fuel, branded 3D-Ultra, is used to build hybrid rocket engines and solid rocket motors whose fuel-grain geometry is printed rather than cast, cutting production timelines from years to weeks while removing the handling hazards of traditional energetics.

The company works under U.S. Army Applications Laboratory SBIR Phase III and U.S. Air Force Test Center contracts, has flight-tested an additively manufactured GMLRS-class analog, and is expanding manufacturing capacity in Lawton, Oklahoma and Crawford, Mississippi.

Firehawk now operates publicly as **Firehawk Defense** (firehawkdefense.com); the firehawkaerospace.com domain 301-redirects there.
